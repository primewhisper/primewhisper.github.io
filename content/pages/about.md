## About this blog

PrimeWhisper is a blog about security research, cryptography, and the places where they meet. Expect deep dives into the math behind privacy-preserving protocols, reverse engineering and low-level security work, and what we can learn from taking AI and machine learning systems apart.

The name comes from two ideas: the prime numbers at the foundation of modern cryptography, and the whisper, a quiet voice of critical thinking in a loud world of hype. The goal is to look closely, verify, and not follow claims blindly.

## About me

Shahar Shechter is a vulnerability researcher at Cyera, specializing in reverse engineering, low-level systems development, and applied cryptography, with additional experience in model training and data science. Previously, he spent three years at Argus (later PlaxidityX) researching vehicle security vulnerabilities, presenting at JSAE 2023 in Japan, and a year at Okta.

He recently completed a master's degree in computer science at Tel Aviv University, focused on multi-party computation and private information retrieval, and presented his work at IACR Crypto 2026. His thesis defense is the last remaining step.
